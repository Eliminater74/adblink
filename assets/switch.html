Switch View
Switch View toggles between enabling adblink's Kodi features or disabling them. If you're using adblink for a device that doesn't have Kodi, this will remove Kodi-related clutter from your view.

You can also set your preferred starting view via preferences

If you have questions or comments, please email jocala@jocala.com or visit one of the forums linked below. adbLink produces a log file, adblink.log, with each run. Include it in your email or post if you're having problems.
